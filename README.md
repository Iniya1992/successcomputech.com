# Success Computech - Customized Gifts Website Setup

A modern, responsive e-commerce web application tailored for **Success Computech** (Personalized Gifts & Printing, Tiruppur), inspired by [Modern Gifts](https://moderngifts.in/).

## Features

### 1. Customer Storefront (Viewer Page)
- **Announcement Bar:** Rating badges, express delivery notice in Tamil Nadu / India, location in Tiruppur.
- **Hero Banner:** Premium typography, call-to-actions, and trust statistics (5,000+ gifts crafted, 4.9★ rating).
- **Categories Navigation:** Acrylic Frames, Photo Frames & Collages, Personalized Mugs, Metal & QR Keychains, Luxury Hampers, Photo Puzzles, Lamps & Light Stands.
- **Budget Filters:** Instant filtering by budget (Under ₹199, Under ₹499, Under ₹799, Under ₹999).
- **Occasion Tabs:** Filter by Birthday, Anniversary, Romantic / Love, Friends & Family.
- **Product Customizer Modal:** Real-time preview with fields for Recipient Name(s), Custom Song / Spotify Code, Date, and Photo Upload instructions.
- **Seamless WhatsApp Ordering:** Generates pre-formatted WhatsApp order messages sent directly to your phone number.
- **Shopping Cart Drawer:** Multi-item cart calculation, delivery address input, and checkout.

### 2. Admin Control Center (Admin Options)
- **Top KPI Counters:** Live product count, total orders, estimated store revenue, and WhatsApp status.
- **Product Management:** Add new gift items, edit prices/discounts, update descriptions, upload image URLs, toggle stock status, and delete items.
- **Order Management:** View incoming orders placed by customers with names, phones, addresses, and customization details. Update production statuses (`Order Placed`, `Photo Received`, `In Production`, `Dispatched`, `Delivered`).
- **Store & WhatsApp Settings:** Configure business name, tagline, WhatsApp number, UPI ID, Tiruppur studio address, and banner text.
- **Local Persistence & Backup:** Built-in `localStorage` synchronization and JSON data backup/export.

## How to Run & Deploy
1. Simply double-click `index.html` to open it in any web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari).
2. To host online for free, you can drag and drop this folder onto **Netlify Drop**, **Vercel**, or **GitHub Pages**.
